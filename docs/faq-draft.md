## Care

**Q: How should I wash my towels?**
A: Machine wash cold with like colours, tumble dry low and take them out promptly. Skip the
fabric softener — it coats the fibres and makes towels less absorbent.
*Source: Care Guide.*
*Keywords: washing, laundry, care, dryer, softener, bleach*

**Q: Should I wash towels before using them?**
A: Yes — washing new towels before first use sets the weave and improves absorbency.
*Source: Care Guide.*
*Keywords: first wash, new towels, before use*

**Q: Do your towels shrink?**
A: A little on the first wash, which is normal for cotton and part of how the weave settles.
Long-staple cotton softens with every wash, so they get better with use.
*Source: Care Guide (softening). **The shrinkage sentence needs your confirmation — I have no
source for how much.***
*Keywords: shrink, size after washing, smaller*

**Q: Can I put bath rugs in the washing machine?**
A: NEEDS YOUR ANSWER — the care guide covers textiles generally but doesn't call out rugs with
backing. Say whether the non-slip backing is machine washable and at what temperature.
*Keywords: wash rug, machine, backing, tumble dry*

## Account

**Q: Do I need an account to order?**
A: No. You can check out as a guest. An account just lets you see your orders, track them and
save addresses.
*Source: checkout does not require sign-in.*
*Keywords: account, guest, register, sign up*

**Q: How do I sign in? I don't remember a password.**
A: There isn't one. Enter your email and we send you a six-digit code — that's the whole
sign-in. You can also use Sign in with Google.
*Source: /account/code and /account/verify are code-based; Google sign-in exists.*
*Keywords: password, login, sign in, forgot, code*

**Q: How do I stop the emails?**
A: Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom, and you can turn off emails in your
account under notifications.
*Source: List-Unsubscribe headers and the account notifications page.*
*Keywords: unsubscribe, emails, newsletter, stop, opt out*
